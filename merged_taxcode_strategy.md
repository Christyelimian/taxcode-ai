## 🎨 DESIGN SPECIFICATIONS

### Homepage Design Philosophy

**Balance:**
- 60% Institutional (Authority, Trust, Education)
- 40% Technology (Innovation, Tools, Modernity)

**Above the Fold:**
- Clean hero with subtle animation
- Clear value proposition
- Prominent CTAs for both tracks
- Trust indicators visible

**Scroll Experience:**
- Logical flow: Educate → Empower → Engage
- Alternating layouts (left-right, grid, full-width)
- Generous white space
- Sticky navigation on scroll

### Typography Hierarchy

```
H1 (Hero Headline): 48px, Bold, Navy
H2 (Section Titles): 36px, Bold, Navy

Body Large: 18px, Regular, Gray

Small: 14px, Regular, Light Gray
```
